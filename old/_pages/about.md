## Research

My research asks how to make AI **trustworthy for clinical use**. I believe trust cannot be added after training; it must be designed across the whole machine learning pipeline, from the data a model learns from, to what it represents internally, to the decisions it makes. My work addresses each stage:

* **Data-level reliability: _which samples and inputs can we trust?_** Training dynamics can flag unreliable samples, so data can be audited before a model is validated. I detect noisy labels from class-wise gradient trajectories ([GradTrack, NeurIPS 2026](/publications/)) and measure whether vision-language models recognise ambiguous visual inputs ([Visual Semantic Entropy, ECCV 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31407)).
* **Representation-level analysis: _does the model learn meaningful clinical patterns?_** Linking a model's internal features to human-understandable concepts makes the evidence behind its predictions inspectable, e.g. adaptive concept bottlenecks for diagnosis ([AdaCBM, MICCAI 2024](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/045-Paper3895.html)) and sparse-autoencoder concepts in audio LLMs ([AR&D, ICASSP 2026, Oral](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22253)).
* **Decision-level faithfulness: _can clinicians understand and trust a prediction?_** Explanations should reflect what actually drove the model's decision, so clinicians can verify it, through probabilistic attribution maps ([CAPE, CVPR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02388)) and faithful counterfactual explanations ([Mirror-CFE, ICCV 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16822)).

I apply these ideas with clinicians and domain experts, including respiratory imaging for children with cystic fibrosis, tumour synthesis for 3D brain MRI (DICTA 2025, Oral), anomaly detection in leukaemia blood cells with haematologists at SA Pathology, and, next, **trustworthy AI for cardiovascular imaging**.

Earlier, I worked on **3D computer vision and continual learning** for point clouds ([ECCV 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15225), [IJCV 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11367)) with [Dr. Shafin Rahman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Pe8C-SUAAAAJ&hl=en), and on graph algorithms ([KDD 2024](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3637528.3672066)) with [Dr. Ahsanur Rahman](https://sites.google.com/site/rahmanmahsanur), at [North South University](http://www.northsouth.edu/), where I received my **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (Summa Cum Laude)**.
